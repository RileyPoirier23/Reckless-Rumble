# DRIVEBOSS

*Fast & Furious meets Papers, Please, set in a city that gets a real winter.*

You're Leo Covington, 19, and you just inherited your dead father's garage. One night you speed through the wrong part of town and wreck a gang's car, and now the garage is their front. Run the counter (stamp it, fail it, or swap the VIN), flip cars off MarketThing, learn to drag and drift from parody legends, race the crews that own the city, and survive eight years and eight winters. Find out the crash that killed your dad was no accident.

A 506Studios game, from the makers of CAGE BOSS.
